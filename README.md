# ✨ Jangkhng 3D NeoTab - Chrome Theme & New Tab Dashboard

Welcome to **Jangkhng 3D NeoTab**, an exclusive, futuristic 3D glassmorphic theme and new tab dashboard created to give your Google Chrome a sleek, dynamic, and high-tech experience.

---

## 🌟 Overview

Jangkhng 3D NeoTab transforms your standard blank new tab page into a personalized, beautiful 3D workspace. It combines futuristic visuals with everyday browsing productivity tools while keeping the browser ultra-smooth and fast.

---

## ✨ Features

* **🎨 3D Futuristic Design:** Clean glassmorphic panels, specular glowing borders, and smooth ambient 3D particles.
* **🖼️ Dynamic Wallpapers:** Auto-rotating online wallpapers across multiple categories (Cyberpunk, Space, Nature, Minimal, Anime) plus local image upload slideshows.
* **🔖 Customizable Bookmarks:** Choose where your bookmarks live — top bar, bottom dock, sidebars, or hidden for maximum focus.
* **⚡ 3D Shortcuts Grid:** Quick access to your favorite websites with interactive 3D cards.
* **🔍 Dynamic Capsule Search:** Instant search engine switching (Google, YouTube, GitHub, DuckDuckGo, Bing, Wikipedia, Perplexity) with quick hotkeys (`/` or `Ctrl + K`).
* **👤 Real-Time Google Integration:** Direct access to your signed-in Google profile, account management tools, and the official 9-dot Google Apps launcher right from your new tab.
* **⚙️ Complete Personalization:** Full control over blur, brightness, 12h/24h clock, shortcuts layout, and greeting name.

## 📥 How to Install in Google Chrome

1. Download or clone this repository to your computer.
2. Open **Google Chrome** and navigate to `chrome://extensions/`.
3. Enable **Developer mode** using the toggle switch in the top-right corner.
4. Click **Load unpacked** in the top-left corner.
5. Select this folder.
6. Open a new tab (`Ctrl + T`) and enjoy your new 3D dashboard!
